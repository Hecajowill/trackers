My profile

Auto-sync

Every session you save is sent to Coach's sheet automatically. If you have no signal, it waits on your phone and sends the next time you open the app.

Send my data to Coach

Not needed when auto-sync is working. Use it if Coach asks, or as a backup: it copies all your sessions as a spreadsheet-ready block.

Team leaderboard

When Coach sends a leaderboard code, paste it here. Your Progress tab will show where you rank on the team and in your class.

Backup & restore

Your log lives only on this phone and this browser. If you switch phones or clear your browser, paste your last copied data here to restore it.

Tip: put it on your home screen

iPhone: Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: menu, then "Add to Home screen". Always open it the same way so your log stays in one place.
