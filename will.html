My profile

Auto-sync

Every session you save is sent to the training sheet automatically. If you have no signal, it waits on your phone and sends the next time you open the app.

Copy my data

Copies every session as a spreadsheet-ready block. Paste it into a note, email or text to yourself once a week as a backup, or share it with a coach or trainer.

Backup & restore

Your log lives only on this phone and this browser. If you switch phones or clear your browser, paste your last copied data here to restore it.

Tip: put it on your home screen

iPhone: Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: menu, then "Add to Home screen". Always open it the same way so your log stays in one place.
